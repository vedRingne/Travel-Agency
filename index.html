About Our Agency

Committed to Safe Highway Travel

Founded in 2019 in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Shree Renuka Travels was established with a singular mission: to provide comfortable, punctual, and safe intercity cab travel across all over Maharashtra — connecting Sambhajinagar to Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Shirdi, Kolhapur, Nagpur, and every district across the state.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence on Maharashtra's highways. Our fleet of meticulously maintained Sedans (Toyota Etios), Ertigas, Toyota Rumions, and Kia Carens are piloted by polite, background-verified chauffeurs who are trained in defensive driving and night highway safety across all state and national highway corridors.
